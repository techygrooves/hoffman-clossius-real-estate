# Journal posts

Add one Markdown file per article. The filename becomes the URL slug
(`market-notes-q1.md` → `/blog/market-notes-q1/`).

```yaml
---
title: 'Article title'
description: 'One or two sentences used in listings and meta description.'
publishedAt: 2026-03-04
author: martin-hoffman # or maryellen-closius
category: market # market | buying | selling | communities | relocation
heroImage:
  src: /images/blog/example.jpg
  alt: 'Describe what the photograph shows.'
draft: false
---
```

Articles are written with the client. Do not generate market commentary,
statistics or predictions without a citable source.
