A deliberately short placeholder. It has one heading, so the template does not
generate a table of contents — that only appears once an article has two or
more top-level sections.

## Why two samples

The long one exercises the contents list, the heading hierarchy and the
typographic range. This one exercises the layout without them, and shows the
"updated" date beside the published date.

Both disappear from a production build. Neither says anything about the market.
